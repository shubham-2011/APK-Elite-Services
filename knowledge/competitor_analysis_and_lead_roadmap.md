## 2. Detailed Competitor Profiles

### Dirtblaster Cleaning Services — The #1 Direct Threat
- **Website:** dirtblaster.in (WordPress/Sydney theme)
- **Reviews:** 2,100+ @ 4.9★ on Google
- **Phone:** 7350321321 / 8888086179
- **Location:** Hadapsar (behind Amanora Mall)
- **Pricing:** 1BHK ₹2,999 (unfurnished) / ₹3,500 (furnished); 2BHK ₹3,500/₹4,500; 3BHK ₹4,500/₹6,000
- **Lead Gen:** WhatsApp float + quote form + "Pay After Service" + click-to-call
- **USP:** Highest review count in Pune, herbal pest control, 3-in-1 packages
- **Weakness:** Dated WordPress site, no video content, no price calculator
- **How to beat:** Modern website (APK already has this), match pricing transparency, outpace reviews

### Urban Company — The 800-lb Gorilla
- **Website:** urbancompany.com (app-first)
- **Reviews:** Millions nationwide
- **Pricing:** 1BHK ₹3,199–₹3,999; 3BHK ₹5,499–₹6,999; Villa ₹8,000–₹12,000+; Sofa ₹249/seat
- **Lead Gen:** Native app, Google/Meta ads (massive budget), push notifications, auto-booking
- **USP:** Instant booking, ₹10,000 damage insurance, 7-day reclean guarantee
- **Weakness:** Sends random gig workers, inconsistent quality, no personal relationship
- **How to beat:** "100% in-house vetted staff" messaging, personal touch, custom B2B quotes

### HiCare (Rentokil PCI) — Pest Control Specialist
- **Website:** hicare.in
- **Reviews:** 4.0–4.5★ (mixed due to support issues)
- **Pricing:** Cockroach ₹999–₹1,499 (single); AMC ₹2,200–₹3,200/yr; Bed bugs ₹3,690+; Termite ₹4,500–₹8,500+
- **Lead Gen:** Interactive price calculator, pincode booking, toll-free, online payment discounts (15–40%)
- **USP:** HACCP certified, digital service reports, pet/child-safe chemicals
- **Weakness:** Pest-only, no cleaning services
- **How to beat:** Offer cleaning + pest combo (one vendor for everything)

### Vijay Home Services — Most Complete Competitor
- **Website:** vijayhomeservices.com
- **Reviews:** 4.6★ (thousands)

### WhatsApp Marketing Benchmarks (India 2026)
- Open rates: 90–98% (vs 20% for email)
- Response time: < 3 min average
- Conversion rates: 12–18% standard, 40%+ for Click-to-WhatsApp ads
- ROI: 3x–70x documented
- Cost per lead (CTWA ads): 30–50% lower than website forms

### Google Ads Benchmarks (Pune Cleaning Market)
- CPC: ₹15–120 depending on keyword competitiveness
- Conversion rate: 5–10% with optimized landing page
- Recommended starting budget: ₹30,000–50,000/month
- Strategy: Start Maximize Clicks → switch to Target CPA after 30+ conversions
- Critical negative keywords: jobs, career, vacancy, salary, free, DIY, how to clean, car wash, laundry, machines, supplier

### Local Services Marketing Funnel (India)
1. **TOFU (Awareness):** Google Maps, Google Ads, Instagram Reels, Facebook video ads
2. **MOFU (Trust):** Locality pages, pricing charts, Google review badges, chemical safety badges
3. **BOFU (Convert):** 1-tap WhatsApp, click-to-call, instant rate estimator, "Pay After Service"
4. **Post-Funnel (Retain):** WhatsApp feedback 24hr post-service, 90-day re-booking prompts, seasonal campaigns
